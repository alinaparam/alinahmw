Part 1Future Continuous

Сначала повтори правило — подглядывай в него, когда делаешь задания. В каждом задании ответить можно только один раз.

will be + -ing

will / won’t be + verb-ing
+I’ll be working at 10 tomorrow.
−She won’t be working on Saturday.
?Will you be working at 8? — Yes, I will. / No, I won’t.
Подсказкиthis time tomorrow, at 8 p.m. tonight, this time next week, in ten years, when you arrive…

When?

  1. Процесс в момент будущегоThis time tomorrow, I’ll be lying on the beach.
  2. Фон, когда случится что-то другоеWhen you arrive, I’ll be cooking dinner.
  3. Вежливый вопрос о планахWill you be using the car tonight? (= можно я возьму?)
  4. Произойдёт «само собой», по расписаниюI’ll be seeing Anna at work tomorrow anyway.

will do vs will be doing

will do — всё действие целикомФакт, решение, обещание, прогноз: I’ll call you tomorrow. I think it will rain.
will be doing — процесс в конкретный моментDon’t call me at 7 — I’ll be driving.

              Part 2Listening · Mia’s Saturday
              Exercise G

              What will Mia be doing?

              Послушай, как Мия рассказывает о своей субботе, и ответь на вопросы. Слушать можно сколько угодно раз.

                Hi, it’s Mia! Let me tell you about my Saturday, because it’s going to be really busy. At eight in the morning, I’ll be running in the park with my sister. At ten, I’ll be having breakfast at a little café near my flat. At twelve, I’ll be cleaning the apartment, because my parents are coming for dinner. At three, I’ll be shopping for food at the market. At six, I’ll be cooking dinner, and at eight, we’ll be eating and talking with my parents. So please, don’t call me at eight. I won’t be checking my phone! But at eleven, I’ll be lying on the sofa and watching a movie. Call me then!
